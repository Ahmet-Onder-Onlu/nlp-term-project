\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Data and protocol.} We use RETECO v1.1. Hyperparameters ($\lambda$, channel weights,
number of demonstrations) are tuned on the train split; we report dev results once per
configuration. Scoring follows the official protocol: \texttt{pytrec\_eval} \texttt{ndcg\_cut\_10},
per-domain mean, macro-average over 13 domains; topics without a ranking count as zero.
