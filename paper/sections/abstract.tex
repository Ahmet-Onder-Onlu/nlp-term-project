\begin{abstract}
Temporal reasoning-intensive retrieval requires finding passages that are not only topically
relevant but also aligned with the time periods a question depends on, a setting where lexical
retrievers perform poorly (BM25 reaches only 0.115 nDCG@10 on the RETECO Track~1 development set).
We present \sys, our system for Sub-tracks~1a and~1b of SemEval-2027 Task~1 (RETECO).
\sys (i)~extracts temporal and entity metadata from every document and stores it alongside
sparse, dense and knowledge-graph indexes; (ii)~analyses each query with an LLM prompted by
\emph{guidance-distilled} demonstrations that reproduce the benchmark's temporal annotation schema
(intent, anchors, events, year window, retrieval plan); (iii)~retrieves through six complementary
channels fused with weighted reciprocal rank fusion and a temporal-alignment prior; and
(iv)~reranks with a metadata-augmented cross-encoder followed by a temporal-aware listwise LLM.

\end{abstract}
